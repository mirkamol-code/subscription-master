# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and keep harassment, insults and personal attacks out of every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews).

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at **hteg9188@gmail.com**. Reports are handled privately, and the maintainer may remove comments or block contributors who break this code.
